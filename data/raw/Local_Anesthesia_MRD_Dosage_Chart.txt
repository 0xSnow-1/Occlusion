SECTION 1: AGENT-BY-AGENT MRD TABLE

Agent                              Conc.    mg/cartridge   MRD(mg/kg)   Abs.Max(mg)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lidocaine plain                    2%       36 mg          4.4          300
Lidocaine + 1:100,000 epi          2%       36 mg          4.4          300
Lidocaine + 1:50,000 epi           2%       36 mg          4.4          300
Mepivacaine plain                  3%       54 mg          4.4          300
Mepivacaine + 1:100,000 epi        2%       36 mg          4.4          300
Prilocaine plain                   4%       72 mg          8.0          400
Articaine + 1:100,000 epi          4%       72 mg          7.0          N/A (calc by wt)
Bupivacaine + 1:200,000 epi        0.5%     9 mg           1.3          90

NOTES:
  - Cartridge volume assumed = 1.8 mL for all calculations above.
  - AAPD values are intentionally MORE CONSERVATIVE than FDA package-insert
    adult MRDs; use the AAPD pediatric values for all patients under 12
    unless directed otherwise by the supervising dentist/anesthesiologist.
  - Articaine: not recommended in children younger than 4 years old.
  - Bupivacaine: not recommended in children younger than 12 years old
    (long duration of soft-tissue anesthesia -> risk of self-inflicted
    lip/cheek/tongue biting injury in young children who cannot yet
    reliably report numbness).
  - Prilocaine: avoid in patients with anemia or methemoglobinemia risk.

SECTION 2: EPINEPHRINE MAX DOSE (SEPARATE FROM LOCAL ANESTHETIC MRD)

  Healthy patient, no cardiac risk factors ........ 200 micrograms (0.2 mg)
  Patient w/ significant cardiovascular disease .... 40 micrograms (0.04 mg)

  1:100,000 epinephrine cartridge (1.8 mL) contains ~18 micrograms epi
  1:50,000  epinephrine cartridge (1.8 mL) contains ~36 micrograms epi

SECTION 3: WORKED EXAMPLES (weight in kg, dose in mg, cartridges @1.8mL)

  --- Example A: 15 kg child, Lidocaine 2% + 1:100,000 epi ---
  MRD = 15 kg x 4.4 mg/kg = 66 mg
  66 mg < 300 mg absolute ceiling -> 66 mg governs
  66 mg / 36 mg per cartridge = 1.83 cartridges MAX

  --- Example B: 20 kg child, Lidocaine 2% + 1:100,000 epi ---
  MRD = 20 kg x 4.4 mg/kg = 88 mg
  88 mg / 36 mg per cartridge = 2.44 cartridges MAX

  --- Example C: 40 kg adolescent, Mepivacaine 3% plain ---
  MRD = 40 kg x 4.4 mg/kg = 176 mg
  176 mg / 54 mg per cartridge = 3.26 cartridges MAX

  --- Example D: 12 kg child, Articaine 4% + 1:100,000 epi ---
  MRD = 12 kg x 7.0 mg/kg = 84 mg
  84 mg / 72 mg per cartridge = 1.17 cartridges MAX
  (NOTE: articaine generally avoided under age 4 regardless of calculated MRD)

SECTION 4: SIGNS OF LOCAL ANESTHETIC SYSTEMIC TOXICITY (LAST) - ESCALATING

  MILD ............ circumoral numbness, metallic taste, tinnitus,
                     lightheadedness, visual disturbance, agitation
  MODERATE ........ slurred speech, muscle twitching, tremors
  SEVERE .......... generalized tonic-clonic seizure, cardiovascular
                     collapse (progresses from HTN/tachycardia early to
                     hypotension/bradycardia/arrhythmia late)

  MANAGEMENT: stop injection -> call for help/EMS -> support airway,
  breathing, circulation -> administer oxygen -> consider IV lipid
  emulsion ("intralipid") therapy for severe cardiovascular toxicity.
